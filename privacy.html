The short version

Science Calendar collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores nothing about you anywhere except on your own device.

What is collected

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

This is a property of how the app is built rather than a promise about how it is operated: the app contains no networking code at all. There is no server to send data to, and no mechanism by which data could leave your device.

What is stored on your device

Three things, all in the app's own local storage, and all removed when you delete the app:

  • Your settings — the accent theme you picked, whether daily alerts are on, and the time you chose for them.
  • A record of what has been exported — the identifiers of events already added to your calendar, so running the export twice does not create duplicates.
  • Nothing else. No history of what you have read, searched for or opened.

None of this is transmitted anywhere.

Permissions

The app asks for two. Both are optional, both are requested only when you use the feature that needs them, and the app works without either.

  • Notifications — used solely for the daily “On This Day” alert at the time you choose. These are scheduled locally by your device. No push server is involved and nothing about you is sent anywhere.
  • Calendar (write-only) — used solely to add anniversaries you have asked for. This is deliberately the write-only level of access, which means the app is technically incapable of reading your existing calendar. It cannot see your appointments, and does not know what is in your calendar even for its own events — which is precisely why it keeps a local record of what it added.

You can revoke either at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Third parties

There are none. No third-party SDKs, no analytics providers, no advertising networks and no data processors. Nothing is shared or sold, because nothing is gathered.

The event data

All historical content ships inside the app; nothing is fetched at runtime. Entries cite Wikipedia and Wikidata as sources, but those citations are text shown to you — not links the app follows or requests it makes.

Changes

Any revision will be posted here with an updated date. Since the app collects nothing, a change is far more likely to be a clarification than a change in practice.
